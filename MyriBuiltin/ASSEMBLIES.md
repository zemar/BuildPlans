## Doorway transport

- D1 desk lower surround: carry upright, edge-first. Its doorway profile is
  12.75 inches wide by 48.75 inches high; its 45.625-inch width runs through the opening.
- D2-D4, N1-N3 and B1-B3: carry upright. All are at most 24 inches wide and
  47.5 inches high, so they fit face-first as well as edge-first.
- H1 open headboard carcass: carry upright, edge-first, presenting 12.75 inches
  by 72 inches. Its 58.75-inch width runs through the doorway; check approach space.
- F1/F2 bed rails: carry lengthwise through the opening, presenting 3 inches
  by 8 inches; each rail extends 76 inches along the direction of travel.
- F3 foot assembly: carry edge-first, presenting 2 inches by 16 inches.
- Loose desktop and backing panels: carry on edge. Bring the 54-inch slats
  through lengthwise. The mattress travels separately on edge.

The checks include modeled face frames but not unmodeled handles, packing,
or hands. Measure the clear opening with the door open, rather than the door
slab. Hallway turns, approach space on both sides, stairs and vehicle clearance
remain unverified; passing the doorway plane does not confirm the whole route.

## Module construction

Each D/N/B cabinet has its own sides, back, top and purpleheart face frame.
D1 is an open-bottom desk surround with its top at 48.75 inches; the desk top
is installed separately at its original height. D2 fits inside D1, ahead of
its back, and now includes four drawer boxes. The other cabinet modules have
bottom panels. Adjacent modules have doubled sides or horizontal panels;
openings and bridge inset doors have been resized accordingly. Bridge doors
retain 1/8-inch reveals. Drawers and doors can be removed for transport.

The model's Outliner contains a named group for every assembly, with individual
parts nested inside it. Hide other groups to inspect one module. The bed rails,
foot assembly and headboard are separate from the cabinet boxes. Bed slats,
mattress, desk top, lighting, backing infills and ceiling trim install in the room.

## Installation sequence

1. Measure the actual room, floor level, wall straightness, ceiling and complete
   transport route before cutting. Confirm module sizes and hardware allowances.
2. Carry in the boxes individually, with drawers/doors removed as needed.
3. Level and align D1 and N1. Fit D2 and the loose desk top inside D1.
4. Level H1 on the floor and secure it to wall framing before placing the
   bridge cabinets. Stack N2 on N1. Install the desk uppers and N3 upright in their final positions;
   clamp adjacent boxes flush and connect them through their adjoining panels.
5. Set the three bridge boxes on the headboard top cap, then connect their adjacent sides and secure the stack to wall framing.
   Wall connections are still needed for restraint; the thin backs and decorative
   face frames are not structural mounting points.
6. Fasten the bed head rail against the walnut cross brace inside H1, then
   install the remaining bed parts; fit doors/drawers, lighting
   and the separate ceiling scribe strips.

Boxes stop at 95.5 inches, leaving a nominal 1/2-inch ceiling installation gap.
This avoids trying to tip a full-height 96-inch box under a 96-inch ceiling.
The gap is closed with narrow site-fitted purpleheart strips, not a structural
fascia. The wall-wide nominal 128-inch fit still requires field verification;
there is no allowance for an out-of-plumb right wall in the current geometry.

This is an assembly-layout model. Panel joints, fasteners, mounting cleats,
hinges, slides, structural bed connections and load ratings are not detailed.
Select those before making a final cut list. The doorway check is complete;
the rest of the transport route remains unverified. Module weight depends on
actual stock and hardware.

## Headboard and bed-frame fit

The 54-inch mattress opening and two 1.5-inch walnut side rails produce a
57-inch outside bed-frame width. H1 has a 57.25-inch clear opening and 0.75-inch
sides, for a 58.75-inch overall width. This leaves 1/8 inch on each side of the
frame. Side panels and purpleheart stiles are continuous; rail-entry pockets
have been removed. The bridge cabinets match the new bay width.

The bed remains centered at x=75, with the footboard front at y=-80 and the
mattress origin at [48, -78, 16]. The desk and nightstand each give up 3/8 inch
to the wider headboard bay. Their shelves, fronts and frame dimensions follow
the revised widths. Overall installed wall width remains 128 inches.

The bed head rail meets the solid walnut attachment cross brace at y=-2.5.
The brace is 57.25 inches long, 1.25 inches deep and 8 inches high. Bolt locations
and brace-to-carcass joints remain to be specified. The 3/4-inch carcass cap and
its connections still need verification for the bridge load; the model does
not establish a load rating. Secure the assembled stack to wall framing.

Rail stock is modeled at a finished thickness of 1.5 inches. Confirm usable
finished thickness when buying and milling nominal 6/4 walnut before cutting
joints. Slats/ledges span the revised 74.5-inch clear frame length; the mattress
stays 54 by 75 inches. The headboard has only a 1/4-inch walnut plywood back;
the extra thick backing panel has been removed. The solid walnut bed attachment
cross brace remains separate from the plywood back.

## Recessed backs and top mounting braces

All 11 carcasses have a 1/4-inch plywood back with its rear face recessed 1 inch
from the carcass rear edge. Each has a 3/4-inch-thick, 3-inch-tall walnut plywood
mounting brace across the inside width directly below its top, behind the back.
The brace rear is flush with the carcass rear, leaving 1/4 inch between its
front and the back panel. Backs fit between the surrounding panels; shelves
stop at the back's front face. Joinery and fixing holes are not modeled.

Nightstand drawer boxes are now 10 inches deep. The separate desk pedestal
sits ahead of the surround's recessed back, with its front frame at y=-24.
The desktop's rear edge meets that same recessed back. The headboard bed
attachment brace and head rail move forward 1 inch inside the unchanged bed
footprint; side rails, ledges and slat spacing are adjusted to meet them.
Mattress position and the footboard front remain unchanged.
